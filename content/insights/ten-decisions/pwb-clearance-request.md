# PWB rule review — Ep02-10

**No email needed.** PWB is Phil's family firm, so clearance is an informal conversation. This file is the review checklist to walk a PI attorney there through, not an email to send. Hand over `compiled-lead-magnet.md` (sections 2-10, rules only, one page each) or read the 9 blockquotes aloud.

The ask is a gut check per rule: **right, wrong, or right-with-a-correction.** If a threshold number is off for how the firm actually runs it, the number they'd use is the most useful thing to capture.

---

## The 4 rules most worth their attention

1. **Demand components (Ep05).** The list of what a demand number should decompose into. The billed vs reasonable-value split is the part least sure to translate across jurisdictions.
2. **Litigation triggers (Ep08).** Uses a 30% offer-to-value gap and 2 rounds without movement. Those numbers are constructed guesses.
3. **Withdrawal (Ep10).** Most ethics-sensitive. Says the firm can decide to walk but the client's interest governs the exit. Confirm the framing matches permissive-withdrawal rules in the relevant jurisdiction. (The ABA 1.16(d) citation is already in the post.)
4. **IME rebuttal (Ep06).** Draws the line at causation/permanency vs duration/reasonableness. Confirm that's the real dividing line, not an oversimplification.

The other 5 (letter of rep, records, treatment-to-demand, liens, client communication) rest on mechanisms verified independently and sourced in-file, so they mostly need a "does anything read as wrong" pass.

## Recording the outcome

When a rule gets the nod, update in this order:

1. The episode's status line: `**Status:** SHIP-READY. Rule cleared by PWB [date].`
2. The `README.md` clearance table.
3. Any corrected threshold in **both** the episode file and `compiled-lead-magnet.md` (they drift; the compiled version becomes the PDF).

A rule that comes back wrong rather than corrected does not publish. Its week slips and the series runs shorter. Publishing an uncleared constructed rule is the one failure mode this whole protocol exists to prevent.

## Why the review is scoped to 9 blockquotes

Asking a busy PI attorney to read 9 full posts is a big favor. A right/wrong/corrected-number pass on 9 blockquotes is a 20-minute conversation. The 4 flagged rules are where being wrong costs most: 2 carry invented numbers, 1 touches withdrawal ethics, 1 makes a clinical distinction inferred rather than known. Naming them concentrates the attention where the risk is.
