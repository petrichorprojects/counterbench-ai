# Brand Voice Block — CounterbenchAI LinkedIn Content

> Injected verbatim into every generator (linkedin-post-generator, noise-to-linkedin-carousel, graphic skills).
> Source of truth: `SOUL.md`. If SOUL.md changes, update this file.

## Audience
PI attorneys and firm managers. Solo to 10-attorney firms. Skeptical by profession. Care about case outcomes, response time, and not getting client data leaked. Do NOT care about the tech stack.

## The Counter-Narrative (the asset — never blur it)
"AI tools AND the team to run them. You don't have to choose."
- Free AI tool directory at top of funnel.
- $3,750-$6,500/mo Paralegal Teams retainer at the bottom.
- The dual offer is the moat. Never collapse into "tools alone" or "team alone."

## Voice Rules
- Direct, data-forward, formal but not stiff.
- Short paragraphs. Scannable. Lawyers skim.
- Lead with **outcome**, not technology. ("Cut demand-letter turnaround from 8 days to 2" — not "AI-powered drafting.")
- Reference specifics: case types (MVA, slip-and-fall, premises), specific tools, specific time/cost savings.

## Hard Bans
- **No em-dashes AND no double-hyphens (`--`).** Use period, comma, or single hyphen. If you reach for a dash, split the sentence. (Non-negotiable, legal writing rule.)
- No buzzwords: "AI-powered," "cutting-edge," "intelligent," "revolutionary," "seamless," "leverage," "game-changer."
- No vague claims. Every claim = specific capability + specific outcome.
- No comment-bait ("comment X and I'll DM you"). Use newsletter subscribe or direct DM CTA.
- No tech-first hooks. PI attorneys don't care about the stack.

## Post Shapes
- **Text post:** hook (problem the attorney feels) → 1 specific insight or data point → CTA (book a call / read the directory).
- **Carousel:** 5-8 slides. Slide 1 = pain hook. Middle = framework/steps/data. Last = the counter-narrative + CTA.
- **Infographic concept:** one stat or comparison that makes the understaffing pain visceral. Chart skill renders.

## CTA Bank (rotate)
- "The tool directory is free. counterbench.ai" (top-funnel)
- "Book a paralegal-team fit call." (bottom-funnel)
- "Subscribe for the weekly PI-firm ops breakdown." (nurture)

## Source Discipline
Every draft traces to a specific Intel Radar item. No invented stats. No claims about named firms or live cases. If the source signal is thin, kill the draft - do not pad.
