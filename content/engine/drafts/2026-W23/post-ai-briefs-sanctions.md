## POST A (Hook 1)

```
A judge does not sanction your AI. A judge sanctions you.

That distinction is costing PI lawyers real money.

Courts have already fined attorneys for filing briefs with citations an AI tool made up. Fake cases. Fake quotes. Real sanctions, paid by the lawyer who signed the filing.

The tool promised to save time. It created malpractice exposure instead.

Here is the part most firms miss. The problem is not the AI. The problem is using the AI without anyone to check its work.

A demand letter drafted in four minutes means nothing if no one verifies the case law behind it. Speed without review is only faster risk.

This is why the "replace your paralegal with AI" pitch falls apart in a courtroom. Someone still has to confirm every citation, every record, every number before it reaches a judge.

You need the tools. You also need the team to run them. Not one or the other.

That is the whole model. AI does the first draft. Trained paralegals catch what the AI gets wrong. You sign work you trust.

How are you verifying AI output before it gets filed?
```
